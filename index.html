Slip privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Slip helps you keep track of what you buy and pay for: orders, deliveries, returns, warranties, subscriptions, bills, vouchers and tickets. It works on your phone. There is no Slip account and no Slip server holding your data.

What Slip reads

With your permission, and only on your phone, Slip reads:

  • purchase-related emails in the email accounts you connect (Gmail, read-only, or another mailbox over IMAP with an app password you create);
  • emails and PDF files you share with it;
  • on Android, if you turn it on, delivery notifications from couriers and shops in SMS and Viber.

What Slip keeps

Only the facts about a purchase: the store, product names and photos, prices, order and tracking numbers, dates, status and the kind of payment (for example "card", never its number). For a voucher, only the last four characters of its code. For food delivery and rides, only the service, the day and the amount, never what you ordered or where you went.

Slip does not keep the emails themselves. It never keeps your name, address, phone number, card numbers or account numbers, even when an email contains them. What it keeps is stored in an encrypted database on your phone.

What leaves your phone

None of your email content or purchase history is sent to us or to anyone else. Slip connects only to:

  • Google's Gmail API, or your mail provider's IMAP server, to read your mail;
  • shops' own servers, to download product photos and store icons shown in your orders;
  • your own Google Drive, only if you use Backup (see below).

Where your phone has a built-in AI model (Gemini Nano on some Android phones, Apple's on-device model on some iPhones) and you allow it, Slip may use it to read an email it couldn't read otherwise. That model runs on the phone and sends nothing anywhere.

Backup

If you choose to back up, Slip saves its encrypted database to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, locked with a passphrase only you know. Slip can't see your other Drive files. Without your passphrase nobody can open the backup, including us and Google. If you forget it, the backup can't be restored.

Google user data

Slip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show you your purchases and remind you of their dates. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone, not used to train AI models, and not read by people.

Your control

You can disconnect any email account at any time. Settings → Delete everything removes all of Slip's data from your phone. Uninstalling Slip removes it too. You can delete a backup from your Google account's Drive settings (Manage apps).

Children

Slip is not meant for children under 16.
